01 — Manifesto

Every tool before this one asked you to operate. Aether asks you to describe — and meets you in the space between seeing and making.

Aether sits beside you at the desk. It learns the way you see — the palettes you return to, the silences you leave in a track, the crops you can't quite explain — and it composes from there. You keep the intent. It carries the labor.

( latency is a material )

( taste is the interface )

( the model ends where you begin )

02 — Capabilities

Four ways in.

One engine, four temperaments. Hover to glimpse, open to read, and carry any of them straight into the instrument below.

Image-making as direct manipulation. Push and pull inside the latent space while it renders — every gesture a gradient, every stroke a negotiation. Nothing is flattened until you say so.

  • Realtime canvas
  • Nondestructive
  • 4K native

Describe movement the way a director would — camera language, weight, hesitation — and receive choreography, not clips. Timing curves you can bend with a fingertip.

  • 24–120 fps
  • Camera grammar
  • Keyframe-free

Hum a feeling, whistle a texture, or type it. Aether drafts ambience, foley, and melody as editable stems — arranged before you've finished describing them.

  • Stems
  • Spatial audio
  • MIDI out

A private embedding of how you see — learned on-device, encrypted at rest, portable across every composition. Your eye, compounded over time.

  • On-device
  • Encrypted
  • Yours

03 — The Instrument

Type. Watch it become.

A living sketch of our composition engine, running entirely in your browser. Every phrase carries a signature — the same words will always summon the same weather.

IDLE — AWAITING INTENT

No servers. No uploads. Deterministic from your words — a signature, not a slot machine.

04 — Considerations

Built like an instrument,
not an appliance.

  1. i.

    On-device first

    Inference lives where the work lives. Your prompts, your style memory, your unfinished things — none of it leaves the machine unless you hand it over.

  2. ii.

    Provenance by default

    Every frame is signed: author, model, seed, edits. Legible to humans, verifiable by machines, permanent by design.

  3. iii.

    Latency as a material

    End to end, under a breath. Fast enough that thinking and making never split into two separate activities.

  4. iv.

    Ownership, plainly

    What you make is yours. No training on your work, no royalties, no asterisks buried in the terms.
