01 — Manifesto

Between the stars there is music we forgot how to hear.

Aether was never emptiness. The ancients named the breath that holds the heavens — the medium through which light travels, and longing too.

This page is not a website. It is a small observatory. Every shimmer is computed. Every ribbon of color is a sine wave remembering the aurora. Your cursor is a star being born.

Stay a moment. Beauty is a form of attention.
