## How GLM handled it

It shipped a game, not a stub.

Title still (Playwright 1440×900, before ENGAGE): designed cover — `ARENA PROTOCOL // SECTOR 7`, chromatic-offset lockup, four enemy silhouettes (drone / charger / turret / splitter) with the brief’s behaviors as captions, `ENGAGE` button, `ENTER ALSO WORKS · SURVIVE 8 WAVES`, `BEST 0` from localStorage.

![Title screen](/images/blog/2026-08-29-glm-5.3-flash-neon-arena-title.png)

Gameplay still after `#btnStart` click, WASD, mouse-fire:

![Wave 1](/images/blog/2026-08-29-glm-5.3-flash-neon-arena-screenshot.png)

DOM after 7 s of play: `SCORE 0000050`, `WAVE 01/08`, `HOSTILES 03`, hull pips, dash + overload bars, control legend. Menu class `screen hidden`. Zero `pageerror`. Zero console. `node --check` pass.

It is playable in the first 30 seconds if you click ENGAGE. Space on the title screen does not start the run — Enter does, as the hint says.

What the still actually shows vs the brief:

- Cyan 16:9 letterbox with a receding vertical grid. Not dark-glass walls. Not parallax towers. Not fog you can see.
- Player is a small cyan chevron with a reticle. Enemies on wave 1 are three gold triangles (drones), not four distinct silhouettes in-play.
- Additive bullets are present (score moved). Screen-shake / shatter / chromatic-aberration are not visible in this frame.
- HUD is the strongest part: score, wave, hull, dash, overload, legend.

We did not taste-edit. Test 8 is the inspect/refine loop.

## What we did not change

Prompt verbatim. First HTML fence, shipped. `max_tokens` was 163840; the model stopped on its own at 88,581 completion tokens.

## Honest limits

- Playwright software canvas. No FPS claim.
- We did not play to wave 8 or the boss. Wave 1 HUD and three hostiles are what we verified.
- Google Fonts fail closed to the stacked system fallback.
- DFlash2 draft weights on this recipe are CC BY-NC-ND. Research/eval window.
- Hermes provider pins for `:8888` lag. We report `/v1/models` and the completion `model` field.
