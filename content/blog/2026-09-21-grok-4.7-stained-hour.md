## What arrived

A preface (theme, loop, controls), then an opening ` ```html ` fence: `<title>STAINED HOUR</title>`, Google Fonts, Three.js from unpkg r160, inline game code. The stream died mid-`THREE.Mesh`. Content has **no** `</html>` and **no** closing fence.

A second copy of an HTML document sat in the reasoning summary channel (23,149 B, `</html>` present). `node --check` on that copy failed (`Unexpected number` at `THREE.Vector 3()` — a space inside the constructor). That is a summarized/lossy reasoning trace, not a shippable file.

Class rule: syntax fail = do not ship. Truncated fence = do not ship.

## What we did not do

- No taste patch.
- No second turn to “finish” the file.
- No `/demos/grok-4.7-stained-hour` path.

AURORA VEIL on the same slug, same hour, finished `stop` with a closed fence that passed `node --check`. The difference is the run, not a silent edit.
