In ["Genre — Science Fiction: Soft SF"](/blog/genre-science-fiction-soft-sf) I said a culture that costs on page twelve has to keep costing on page three hundred, including during the love scene. I asked you for Document I — want, method, cost — and a four-hundred-word reunion that did not get a confession, a first-name, or a private hour. If you did that work, you hired an institution. Today we take it off the town and ask whether it survives distance.

The cheap definition of scale is more. More planets. More languages. More technologies. That definition will ruin the book, because more is what the model is for. Completeness at scale is a wiki with orbits. A wiki is a completeness machine. Completeness is how Soft SF dies in a series: the caste that bound the kitchen is explained, then translated, then refunded two cities later because the prompt was a new room and the prior is thick with functioning worlds.

Scale is not more worldbuilding. Scale is the problem of which refusals travel. The mill whistle does not need to exist on the next continent. The drought's want might become a water law. If you cannot tell those apart, you will either import the whole town into every chapter or generate a fresh brochure at every border. Both are refill. One of them looks like continuity. The other looks like richness. Neither is a second witness.

By the end you should be able to name the constraints that travel, leave the rest behind, keep Document I from becoming a constitution nobody reads, and catch the model's planetary completeness before it becomes lore.

## More Is a Brochure with Orbits

Ask a model for science fiction at scale and you will get a map. Named systems. One-paragraph ethnographies. A technology tree. A Council. Three religions distinguished by color. That is the prior for "epic." It is also the brochure from ["Novel II — Worldbuilding: Setting as Character"](/blog/novel-ii-worldbuilding-setting-as-character), printed once per planet.

Herbert did not scale Arrakis by describing Caladan in the same density. Arrakis is a want: water unused by strangers, open air as a cost of speech, a stillsuit that does not come off because the scene is intimate. The Imperium is that want given institutions — CHOAM, the Landsraad, a spice monopoly that makes the drought political. Caladan is a contrast, not a second encyclopedia. If you generate Caladan as a complete ecology, you have written a different book, one the model is happier to write.

Le Guin's Hainish novels are the other lesson people misread. The Ekumen is a traveling constraint: you may visit; you may not annex the physics. Gethen remains winter. She did not fill eighty worlds. She let one diplomatic refusal meet one local physics, then stopped. You are writing a novel whose second witness has to survive a border.

In ["Novel II — Worldbuilding: Maps, Timelines, Glossaries"](/blog/novel-ii-worldbuilding-maps-timelines-glossaries) I said an instrument is small enough to check. Five places. A column of dates. Twelve words. Scale does not raise those numbers by an order of magnitude. Scale asks which of them are floor, and which were only the kitchen.

Foundation is sometimes taught as the encyclopedia novel. It is not. Psychohistory is a floor: the future has a grain you can violate locally and not globally. Each episode is a local payment against that grain. The planets are not complete. Generate "a complete timeline of the Galactic Empire" and you will get kings so the dates have somewhere to sit. Those kings will leak. A character will think, helpfully, about the succession. You have seen this leak. It is the encyclopedia smuggle with a starmap.

## What Travels

You do not need a taxonomy to write. You need to know, before you generate the scene two cities away, which facts are allowed to stay behind.

Floor physics travels. The well is dry; orogeny is not a metaphor; kemmer is a cycle; names cost; spice is not flavor. If you change the floor because a new planet would be prettier without it, you swapped books. Readers who have been paying attention will feel the floor move. They will not name the problem as "scale." They will name it as the series going soft.

Regional politics travels only if someone pays. Who owns the water. Which title is an insult. Whether the accident may be spoken. These can change at a border. They cannot change because the prompt was in a softer register, or because book two needed a love scene and the caste was inconvenient. A caused change is plot: she crosses into a city where the title is a job, and we watch what the insult does when it stops working. An uncaused change is refill with a passport.

Local texture must not travel. The mill whistle, the name of the inn, the color of the tram, the door no one uses. Texture that was used is physics *in that place*. It is not physics in the next place. Importing it is a continuity tic: the model, or you, treating one town as the universe. Leaving it behind is how scale is felt. She does not hear the whistle. She hears something else, and the something else has its own cost, or it is scenery and you do not linger.

Did this constraint appear on the page already, in a way the reader will remember? Then it is promoted. Promoted floor travels. Promoted texture does not, unless the character carries it — a stillsuit in a humid city is interesting; a mill whistle in a humid city is a glitch.

Can you point to the scene where a political cost was paid to change it? If the answer is "it is implied by the distance," it is not implied. Distance is not payment. Distance is a prompt boundary. The model treats prompt boundaries as permission to complete.

Would striking it from the new scene damage the book's floor, or only the memory of the kitchen? If only the kitchen, leave it. Scale is a sequence of leavings.

*Ancillary Justice* understands this. The Radch is floor: annexation, ancillary bodies, a language that does not gender. Local stations have customs that do not get a chapter of ethnography. The ship is the scale mechanism — one consciousness, many rooms — not a wiki of the galaxy. Leckie did not need twenty worlds. She needed one traveling refusal and the rooms it could not make identical.

*A Memory Called Empire* does the same with language. Teixcalaan travels because Mahit carries it in her mouth. Lsel stays local. Generate "the complete culture of Teixcalaan" and you will get festivals. Martine gave you a poem that can kill a career. That is enough.

## The Completeness Accent at Distance

Here is the AI accent of fake scale, and it is more specific than "the model forgets the last planet."

The model does not forget. Each prompt is a new room. If the note on the door does not say that the well is dry and the title is an insult, the new city has water and first-names, because functioning cities are the prior. Special cases expire when the prompt ends. I said this about wells in ["Novel II — Worldbuilding: Consistency Engines"](/blog/novel-ii-worldbuilding-consistency-engines). It is worse with borders, because borders, in the training data, are where new furniture arrives.

Four refill types show up the moment you leave town.

Imported kitchen. The mill, the whistle, the dusk patrols, the unspoken accident: all of them appear in the new city because you pasted Document I into the prompt, or because you asked for "continuity." Continuity, to the model, means repeating nouns. Repeating nouns is not scale. It is the town with a different sky.

Fresh brochure. You did not paste Document I. The model supplies a functioning environment: a market, a marriage custom, a technology, a proverb, a Council. A world under constraint looks, to an assistant, like an incomplete world. The assistant completes it. Completeness ethnography, once per stop. Your series now has twelve cultures and no physics.

Convenience technology. Distance is a cost. The model will refund it. Gates, ansible, shuttles that arrive when the scene is ready, translators that dissolve the hard word. A stillsuit is technology that costs. A transporter in every city is a creek behind the kitchen. Scale's reader will catch a journey that took no time and name it as the book going soft.

Series amnesia. Book two is a new prompt. The caste that bound the kitchen is now a theme. Magic that cost in book one is a metaphor by book two. This is Wednesday's taboo holiday, stretched across a publication schedule. You hired a floor. A sequel that spends the floor and then refunds it has no floor.

In ["Hallucination, Confabulation, and the Limits of Generation"](/blog/hallucination-confabulation-limits) I said the model will invent a plausible fact rather than admit a gap. Scale inconsistency is that habit pointed at your own map. The invention is locally plausible. It is globally a lie about a distance you already decided.

Do not fix this by pasting the galaxy bible into every prompt. The model will smuggle. A character will think, helpfully, about orbital policy in language no one at the well would use. You have seen this leak. It is the encyclopedia smuggle wearing a navigator's coat. Chapter nine gets the scene prompt and the two or three constraints that actually touch these pages. The bible stays on your desk.

## A Constitution Nobody Reads

Wednesday I said a two-hundred-page ethnography is a wiki. Scale tempts you into a constitution: Document I grown into articles, exceptions, planetary amendments. You will not open it. An unread constitution is wallpaper with a preamble.

Keep the traveling ledger small enough to check. After each chapter, model closed, six lines:

What still wants, at floor level — the want that would make this a different book if it vanished.

Which floor constraints appeared on the page.

Which of those are now promoted.

Whether anyone paid a political cost at the border, and what the new cost is.

What you left behind — local texture that must not follow.

Where the model, or you, refilled: imported kitchen, fresh brochure, convenience technology, series amnesia.

That last pair is the one people skip. Leaving is a decision. Refill is a decision you pretend was richness. Write both down.

Keep the ledger next to Document I, not inside it. Document I is the hiring portrait of one institution. The ledger is the employment record of distance. Mixing them produces a bible that grows in every direction and cannot be checked. You already know what happens when you put that bible in the prompt.

A miniature, because the method is easier to steal than the slogan. Document I says: the accident is unspoken; kinship includes the mill-owner; the title is an insult; dusk patrols do not thin. The kitchen reunion, if you did Wednesday's homework, happened under those constraints. Now you generate four hundred words two cities away, or a generation later. What arrives, if you are not careful, is one of two kitchens from stock. Either the whistle is there, the title is there, the accident is still the local secret — imported kitchen. Or there is a new caste, a new festival, a translator, and a private hour — fresh brochure. Strike both. Carry the floor: the accident remains unspoken *in her mouth*, even if this city names it; the title is still an insult *to her*, even if it is a job here. Leave the whistle. Leave the mill window. Let this city's cost be something the ledger can hold in a line, not a paragraph of ethnography. The scene now has to happen under a traveling refusal and a local silence. That is scale. The other was a travel chapter from the prior.

A caused change is still allowed. Someone says the word in the new city; we watch what the old mouth does. The stillsuit comes off because the air is wet and we have already paid for that wetness in the journey. The institution is allowed to lose at a border. It is not allowed to have been kidding, and it is not allowed to be replaced by a complete culture the prompt invented overnight.

You may use the model as a checker once the chapter exists. Paste the chapter and the six-line ledger from the previous chapter — not the constitution — and ask where the world got easier than the ledger allowed, and where last chapter's kitchen walked in. Do not ask "is this consistent." Consistent, to the model, means locally smooth or globally repeated. Smooth is the fresh brochure. Repeated is the imported kitchen. Then you verify. Do not accept a proposed new planet, custom, or device from the checker. The checker is a sniffer. It is not a cartographer.

In ["First Draft by AI, Revision by Human — A Workflow"](/blog/first-draft-by-ai-revision-by-human-a-workflow) I said the human pass is where decisions live. Scale is a decision against the model's fluency in worlds. Fluency in worlds is the prior. A universe is a set of refusals that can stand being carried, and a larger set that cannot.

If you skip the ledger and keep only Wednesday's Document I, you can still write the town. You will have a harder time leaving it. A culture that will not refund a marriage can still grow a transporter behind the kitchen. Scale is the leaving. The marriage is only the thing she carries.

## For Next Time

On Monday we take the same problem into a genre that names its physics as magic: ["Genre — Fantasy & Mystery: Magic Systems"](/blog/genre-fantasy-mystery-magic-systems). A magic system is floor with a costume. Cost, limitation, and the rule you may not refund for tenderness: you have been practicing these on wells, titles, and stillsuits. Fantasy's model-prior is even thicker with convenience. Spells arrive when the scene is ready. The cost is a headache, then it is nothing. We will talk about hiring a system the way you hired an institution, and about catching the refund before it becomes lore.

Your homework until then: take Wednesday's Document I and the three instruments from the worldbuilding week. Model closed, write the six-line traveling ledger. Name three constraints that travel and two you will leave behind. Then generate four hundred words in a second city, or a generation later — a scene that would like a translator, a shortcut, and a complete new custom. Run the ledger. Strike every imported kitchen, every fresh brochure, every device that made the distance free. If the want from the worldbuilding week is still audible, and more expensive because it had to survive a border without becoming a constitution, the scale is working. Bring the ledger on Monday. Magic is wasted on a world that will not keep its own word, and it is merciless on a world whose map was a wiki with orbits.
