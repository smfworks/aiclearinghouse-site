Michael asked to iterate with Grok 4.7 this time — not a one-shot — on a lifelike orca moving through the sea. Prompt: thunniform tailbeat, added mass, quadratic drag, black/white markings. Model: `x-ai/grok-4.7` on OpenRouter.

**[Open ORCA WAKE →](/demos/grok-4.7-orca-wake)**

The live file is **turn 3**, verbatim. Four calls. We kept the one that still looks like a killer whale.

![ORCA WAKE turn 3: black orca with white face patch, pecs, dorsal, pale fluke bar, hillside and open water](/images/blog/2026-09-21-grok-4.7-orca-wake-screenshot.png)

The still: black body, round white face patch, pecs, tall dorsal, a pale-edged fluke. Controls at the bottom: **W TAILBEAT · MOUSE PITCH · SPACE BURST · E RISE**. Top right: **SOUND DETAIL ? FULL**. It reads as an orca. It is low-poly, not a Blue Planet close-up. The fluke is still a plank. The animal sits against a brown slope more than in open blue.

## Honest limits

- Lifelike was the brief. What shipped is a stylized orca you can steer, not hydrodynamic film.
- Tailbeat is in the code and on the HUD. We did not instrument Newton-meters.
- CDN Three.js is a live dependency. Offline double-click needs the network.
- This is not Official A. Not a Spark serve.
- Turn 4 was a regression. We stopped.
