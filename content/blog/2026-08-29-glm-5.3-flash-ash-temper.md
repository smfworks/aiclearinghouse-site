## How GLM handled it

It shipped a product surface you could screenshot as a portfolio piece.

Hero still (Playwright 1440×900):

![Hero](/images/blog/2026-08-29-glm-5.3-flash-ash-temper-screenshot.png)

Soot field, gold nav, italic copper ampersand, a cooling-steel bar through *Temper*, two CTAs. Caption in the DOM: *FIG. 01 — A BILLET OF 1084, COOLING ON THE ANVIL.* Palette is soot / parchment / blood-oxide / pale gold. Not neon.

Work grid:

![Work](/images/blog/2026-08-29-glm-5.3-flash-ash-temper-work.png)

SVG plates with steel type, length, one-sentence provenance (Piedmont Chef from a Pittsboro flea-market plowshare; Eno Skinner lost to the river). Six pieces in the DOM.

Estimator (DOM after scroll, default 14 in / 1084 / walnut / 0 engraving):

- Low **$385** · likely **$430** · high **$520**
- ~7 weeks, week of Oct 17
- Materials list: 1084 billet 14″ × 1.41″ × 0.20″, foil quench, 58–60 HRC
- *How we quote* disclosure present
- Steels: 1084 ×1.00, 80CrV2 ×1.15, O1 ×1.38. Rush ×1.35

That is custom-work money, not $19.99.

![Estimator](/images/blog/2026-08-29-glm-5.3-flash-ash-temper-estimator.png)

Zero `pageerror`. Zero console.

Concrete defects in the stills:

1. Hero *Temper* collides with the glowing bar — type sits in the heat.
2. Estimator still clips the process diagrams under the sticky nav (two gold plates hanging into the estimator heading).
3. Sticky nav has two items underlined (PROCESS and ESTIMATOR) at once.
4. Estimator price card is cut at 1440×900; the $385–$520 band is in the DOM, not fully in the frame.
5. Google Fonts fail closed.

We did not taste-edit. Test 8 is the inspect/refine loop.

## What we did not change

Prompt verbatim. First HTML fence, shipped. The model stopped on its own at 66,944 completion tokens.

- We did not open a spec modal or submit the contact form in the still.

- Hermes provider pins for `:8888` lag. We report `/v1/models` and the completion `model` field.
