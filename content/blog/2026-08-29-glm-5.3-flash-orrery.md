## How GLM handled it

It built a mixed 3D/2D product, not a gray rectangle.

Lid still (Playwright 1440×900, before click):

![Lid](/images/blog/2026-08-29-glm-5.3-flash-orrery-lid.png)

A mustard disc with radial seams and `LIFT THE LID`. That is the iris illustration. It is also a pie chart with no engraving.

After click:

![Open](/images/blog/2026-08-29-glm-5.3-flash-orrery-screenshot.png)

Left: Three.js Earth (procedural continents, terminator, a gold orbital arc, star field). Header `ORRERY MÉCANIQUE · Nº VII`. Time rail −50 Y / EPOCH 2086 / +50 Y, 1× 10× 100×, TELESCOPE. Tracking EARTH.

Right: mission board in engraved type. Dossier SOL III, epithet *The First Clock, Keeper of the Meridian*, cruise @ 0.04 c = 4 H 01 M, 80-word landing note (Larkhill, cut hay). Three posts: Night Survey, Aerostat, Relic — ACCEPT buttons.

Zero `pageerror`. Console: WebGL ReadPixels stall only.

Concrete defects in the stills:

1. Lid is a flat gold pie, not a brass engraving that irises in the first frame.
2. Default tracking is Earth, so the still is a globe, not Mercury–Saturn at visual scale. Other worlds exist in the DOM planet list; they are not in this frame.
3. Continents are a tan blob (Africa-shaped), not crater-noise / banded giants.
4. Mission III is clipped at the bottom of 1440×900.
5. Google Fonts fail closed.

We did not taste-edit. Test 8 is the inspect/refine loop.

## What we did not change

Prompt verbatim. First HTML fence, shipped. The model stopped on its own at 65,493 completion tokens.

## Honest limits

- Playwright software WebGL. No FPS claim.
- We clicked the lid; we did not ACCEPT a mission or rack the telescope in the still.
- DFlash2 draft weights on this recipe are CC BY-NC-ND. Research/eval window.
- Hermes provider pins for `:8888` lag. We report `/v1/models` and the completion `model` field.
