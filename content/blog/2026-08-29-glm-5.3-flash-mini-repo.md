## How GLM handled it

It combined the orrery metaphor with the Ash & Temper brand as a commission calendar. Planets are workstations. Moons would be jobs. The forge is the shop.

Playwright Chromium, 1440×900, served from the folder (relative scripts). Zero `pageerror`. Zero console.

![Orrery](/images/blog/2026-08-29-glm-5.3-flash-mini-repo-screenshot.png)

Five dashed orbits, numbered stations I–V, a glowing forge at the center, a brass `TODAY` meridian. Ledger: Layout Bench, The Saw, Joinery Bench, Assembly, Finishing Room — all **0 pcs / idle**. Transport: pause, 1×/4×/16×, DAY 02, + New commission, mute. Keyboard chips on the bar.

After `H`:

![Help](/images/blog/2026-08-29-glm-5.3-flash-mini-repo-help.png)

On-screen keyboard map: Space, 1–3, arrows, N, M, H, Esc. That was required.

Concrete defects in the still:

1. Queues start empty. The orrery is a clock with no moons until you press N. Test 1 in `tests.md` does not seed jobs.
2. Ticker says *The forge is lit. Day one.* while the ledger and transport say **Day 2**.
3. Finishing Room wraps to two lines in the ledger.
4. Google Fonts fail closed (Fraunces + IBM Plex Mono).

We did not taste-edit. We did not play through all 10 manual tests; we loaded, resized via the viewport, pressed H, captured stills.

## What we did not change

Prompt verbatim. Files split from the model's path-headed fences, not rewritten. `orrey.js` spelling matches the brief (`js/orrey.js`, not `orrery.js`).

## Honest limits

- Playwright software canvas. No FPS claim.
- Empty start is a product choice and a scoring miss on “serious execution” of a calendar.
- DFlash2 draft weights on this recipe are CC BY-NC-ND. Research/eval window.
- Hermes provider pins for `:8888` lag. We report `/v1/models` and the completion `model` field.
